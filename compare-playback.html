Neonic playback: legacy vs. resolution-adaptive

Same source PNG, mounted into canvases of varying CSS size. The legacy column uses the embedded indices buffer and relies on the browser to downsample the bake into the small CSS box. The optimized column re-bakes from the embedded anchors at the canvas's actual display resolution × devicePixelRatio. Compare visual quality, then look at the backing-store pixel count (per-frame compute budget).
