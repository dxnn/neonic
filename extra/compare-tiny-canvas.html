Bake A/B: binary vs. soft-mask

Same anchors, same display canvas, three soft-mask bake strategies across four source PNGs. A bakes at display × dpr × 2 (4× the pixels of C) and relies on GPU downsampling for extra smoothness. B bakes at the legacy quality floor (480 long) — same per-frame compute as the original binary path, just with mask AA. C drops the floor entirely: bake's long edge equals display × dpr × 1 exactly.
