Bake A/B: binary vs. soft-mask

Same anchors, same display canvas, three different bake strategies. A is today's binary bake at the legacy-quality floor (480 long). B keeps the same bake size but swaps to soft-mask compositing — same compute per frame, edges from the mask instead of from browser oversampling. C drops the bake to 200 long (mask handles AA), targeting big compute savings at the same visual quality.
